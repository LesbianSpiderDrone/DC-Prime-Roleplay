## OC Worldbuilding Elements

### Ishtarite Magical Tradition
While Mesopotamian gods exist in DC canon, the specific Ishtarite tradition of sacred sexuality theology as a magical framework is the player's creation. It is practiced by Stephanie Salvage and applied through the System's syncretic methodology.

### Syncretic Magic as Cooperative Integration
The idea that multiple magical traditions can cooperate as equals (rather than being subordinated to a master framework) is the session's central innovation. This is presented as unprecedented in the DCU — a discovery enabled by the System's plural cognitive architecture.

### Property-Based Classification
The System's approach to cataloguing materials by functional properties rather than origin/name is an OC methodology. This approach is what enabled their cross-system discoveries, including Element X.

### Element X Independent Discovery
While Element X is DC canon (*Dark Nights: Metal*), the System's specific method of discovering it — bottom-up property analysis finding trace amounts across unrelated samples — is session-created narrative.
