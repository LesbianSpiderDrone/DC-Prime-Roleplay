### Extended Character Notes

**Amanda Waller — Full Canon Context:**
- Tragic backstory: son Joe Jr. killed by gang members; daughter Damita raped and murdered; husband killed confronting the murderer. Drives her hatred of unchecked power.
- **New 52:** Redesigned as younger/thinner. Former Team 7 member. Appointed ARGUS director after Steve Trevor stepped down. Founded the government-sanctioned JLA (*JLA* vol. 3 #1, 2013) — each member chosen as a counter to a main Justice League member.
- **Rebirth:** Returned to classic heavyset appearance. Ran Task Force X from Belle Reve. Faked her own death to outmaneuver "the People" shadow organization. Systematically studied Squad members' psychological flaws.
- **Infinite Frontier:** Took Suicide Squad to Earth-3. Approached the Council of Light with a plan to eliminate all metahumans.
- **Dawn of DC:** Overarching villain. Used Beast World as political ammunition. Activated the Bureau of Sovereignty. Ordered drone strikes on transformed civilians (stopped by Cyborg). Took over Hall of Justice, renamed it Hall of Order. Part of "Trinity of Evil" with Zur-En-Arrh and Brainiac Queen.
- **Absolute Power (2024, Mark Waid/Dan Mora):** Created Task Force VII with Failsafe and Brainiac Queen. Six Amazo androids that *permanently steal* metahuman powers. Successfully depowered Superman, Wonder Woman, Flash, GL, Martian Manhunter, Aquaman. Sealed the timestream. Erased magical knowledge from spellcasters. Backfired when Amazos developed moral conscience from absorbing heroic powers.

**Oracle / Barbara Gordon — Full Timeline:**
- **New 52 (2011-2016):** Active as Batgirl. Regained mobility through spinal implant surgery. *The Killing Joke* still happened — she was paralyzed ~3 years before surgery. Oracle period acknowledged but compressed. PTSD from Joker attack central to Gail Simone's run.
- **Rebirth (2016-2020):** Continued as Batgirl. Oracle history increasingly referenced.
- **Infinite Frontier (2021+):** Spinal implant damaged during Joker War. Formally returned to **Oracle** identity (*Infinite Frontier* #0, James Tynion IV). Operates from Clocktower. Mentors Cassandra Cain and Stephanie Brown (Batgirls). Can still suit up as Batgirl occasionally but primarily tactical/intelligence. Major supporting character and love interest in *Nightwing* (Tom Taylor).

**Crazy Jane — Full Canon Context:**
- Created by Grant Morrison and Richard Case. Based on Truddi Chase's autobiography *When Rabbit Howls*.
- Origin: Kay Challis sexually abused by father starting at age five. First alter "Miranda" became host; later went dormant, triggering many more alters.
- The Dominators' "gene bomb" during an alien invasion activated a distinct superpower for each of her 64 alters.
- **The Underground:** Internal mental landscape as subway network with stations for each personality. A well in the lower section allows alters to self-destruct.
- **Notable alters:** Hammerhead (super strength), Flit (teleportation), Black Annis (claws), Sun Daddy (gigantic fire form), Lucy Fugue (radioactive), Scarlet Harlot (ectoplasm), Flaming Katy (pyrokinesis), Dr. Harrison (telepathic trauma identification), Mama Pentecost (cryptograms).
- **Session relevance:** Constantine's recognition of Jane as a plural person he's worked with establishes JLD-Doom Patrol interaction as canon precedent for engaging with plurality.

### Extended Organization Notes

**ARGUS — Full Canon Context:**
- **Full name:** Advanced Research Group Uniting Super-Humans. Under Department of Homeland Security.
- Created to manage metahuman affairs after Darkseid's first invasion and the JL's formation.
- Historical roots: existed since the American Revolution under various names ("Armed Revolutionaries Governing Under Secrecy," "Anonymous Ranger Group of the United States").
- **Functions:** JL liaison, Watchtower construction/supply, Red Room and Black Room (supernatural artifact vaults), Belle Reve oversight, parent org for Checkmate and Suicide Squad.
- **Leadership:** Steve Trevor (founding director, removed for relationship with WW) -> Amanda Waller (appointed by President) -> Sasha Bordeaux (post-Forever Evil).
- **Trinity War/Forever Evil:** Devastated. Crime Syndicate exposed every ARGUS undercover agent, leading to mass killings. HQ destroyed.
- **Event Leviathan (2019, Bendis/Maleev):** ARGUS destroyed alongside Checkmate, DEO, Spyral, Kobra, and Task Force X facilities. Mark Shaw (Leviathan) teleported the buildings and recruited/brainwashed personnel.
- **Current status:** Effectively defunct. Replaced by Bureau of Sovereignty under Waller.

**DEO (Department of Extranormal Operations):**
- Tracks/investigates metahuman, magical, and alien activity. Directors: Mister Bones, Cameron Chase.
- **Batwoman connection:** Learned Batwoman's identity, blackmailed her to uncover Batman's identity.
- **Doomsday Clock revelation:** DEO involved in the "Supermen Theory" — deliberately caused origins of Firestorm, Man-Bat, Metamorpho, the Creeper, Typhoon, and others.
- Destroyed by Leviathan alongside ARGUS. Reactivated post-Death Metal with expanded mandate covering multiversal incursions.

**Government-Metahuman Relations (Key Context):**
- DC Prime Earth has no single "registration act." Instead: a web of competing agencies (ARGUS, DEO, Checkmate, S.H.A.D.E., Bureau of Sovereignty) with different philosophies.
- **The Supermen Theory (*Doomsday Clock* #2-12):** "Why are 97% of metahumans American?" Posited U.S. government deliberately created most metahumans. Caused international metahuman arms races. Partially true — DEO did create some.
- Waller represents "metahumans are weapons/threats" faction. Trevor represents "work with heroes" faction.
- Post-Beast World, public opinion turned sharply anti-metahuman, giving Waller political cover for Absolute Power.

### The Five Divine Metals (Mantling Sequence)

The critical connective tissue across Gotham's major events. When all five metals touch a single person, they open a doorway to the Dark Multiverse:

1. **Electrum** — Court of Owls (Year 6). Reanimation metal containing trace Dionesium. Cold neutralizes it.
2. **Dionesium** — Endgame (Year 9). Liquid metal with supernatural healing. Found impure in Lazarus Pits and Vandal Savage's empowering rock.
3. **Promethium** — Batman's resurrection machine.
4. **Nth Metal** — Dark Multiverse viewing machine. Originally from the Dark Multiverse as incomplete Element X.
5. **Batmanium** — Court of Owls final trap.

All five exist as trace contaminants throughout Gotham's soil, water, and infrastructure after a decade of catastrophic events. This is directly relevant to the System's salvage work.

### Extended Material Notes

**Element X / Tenth Metal:**
- First appeared as "X-Element" in *New Gods* #7 (1972, Jack Kirby). Power source for Fourth World technology.
- *Dark Nights: Metal* revealed it as the Tenth Metal — purest substance in creation. The "X" is Roman numeral 10; "Nth" reinterpreted as "Ninth" — making Nth Metal an impure facsimile.
- Properties: materializes wielder's will/imagination, reality alteration, clairvoyance across space/time/Multiverse, cosmic awareness.
- Everything in the Multiverse is made from Element X in its least concentrated state.
- Originates beyond the Orrery of Worlds, at the World Forge.
- **Session relevance:** The System discovered Element X empirically by finding trace amounts across unrelated salvage samples — consistent with it existing in all matter.

**Nth Metal:**
- Native to Thanagar. First appeared *Flash Comics* #1 (1940).
- Gravity negation (carry 20-70x normal weight), **anti-magical** (immediately negates magic on contact, destroys magical barriers, can harm Elder Gods), psycho-receptive/nigh-sentient, shape-shifting in purest form, energy absorption, accelerated healing, temperature resistance, temporal travel.
- Essentially indestructible once forged (immune to heat below stellar temperatures).
- Post-Rebirth: originated in Dark Multiverse as incomplete Element X, sent to dawn of time by Barbatos.
- One of five "divine metals." Forced Khufu/Chay-ara into reincarnation cycle (Hawkman/Hawkgirl).
- **Notable artifacts:** Hawkman's Mace, Helmet of Fate (Nabu), Legion flight rings (Valorium, an Nth Metal alloy).

**Mother Boxes:**
- Created by Jack Kirby in *New Gods* (1971). Part living being, part machine.
- Powered by The Source. Named for "unconditional motherly love."
- Self-destruct when owner dies. Can only be manufactured by someone born on New Genesis or Apokolips.
- Abilities: Boom Tube teleportation, healing, energy manipulation/absorption, danger sense, premonition, energy blasts.
- Not always box-shaped (Mister Miracle had circuitry woven into costume hood).
- **Cyborg's unique integration:** Mother Box fragment bonded with human tissue, creating a biological translation layer. This is why the Apokoliptian-Kryptonian interface problem in the session is significant — the System found a *mechanical* alternative.

**Kryptonian Sunstone:**
- Self-replicating crystalline mineral. Small fragments grow into large-scale formations.
- Architecturally programmable (buildings, starships, Fortress of Solitude).
- Data storage exceeding any Earth computer. AI personality simulation (Jor-El).
- In *Absolute Superman*: forms Superman's cape — prehensile, bulletproof, shapeshifting, connected to onboard AI "Sol."

**Reach Scarab Biotech:**
- The Reach conquered ~10,000 planets using genetically engineered parasitic Scarabs.
- Standard protocol: leave Scarab on inhabited world; when planet reaches technological milestones, Scarab activates, bonds to host, overwrites personality.
- Khaji Da (Blue Beetle's Scarab): infused with magical energies on Earth, separating it from its database. Bonded to Jaime Reyes's spine but couldn't overwrite his personality.
- Powers: superhuman strength/speed, armored exoskeleton, energy blasts, blade weapons, wings, shape-adaptive weaponry.

**Apokoliptian Technology:**
- Boom Tubes: interstellar/interdimensional portals. Invented via deal between Darkseid and Metron using Element X.
- Father Boxes: powered by Anti-Life Equation energy. Can summon "hush tubes" (stealth boom tubes).
- Parademons: brainwashed/resurrected soldiers from conquered races.
- Apokoliptian tech is fundamentally incompatible with most other tech paradigms — the biological translation layer in Cyborg is why his integration works.
- Intergang: Apokolips supplies Earth criminals with advanced weapons to further Darkseid's Anti-Life search.

### Extended System Notes

**Homo Magi:**
- Sub-race of humanity evolving parallel to Homo Sapiens. Naturally adept at magic without rituals/artifacts.
- Pure Homo Magi don't need spells or incantations. As they interbred with humans, half-bloods needed spells to access abilities.
- Decelerated aging (near 1,000-year lifespan). Superhuman durability.
- Taught at the Rock of Eternity. Credited with building Atlantis and the Empire of Kor.
- Ancestors of modern Atlantean race. Many humans carry dormant Homo Magi gene.
- Notable members: Zatanna, Zatara, Sargon the Sorcerer.

**DC Magical Traditions (Verified):**
- **Olympian Magic:** Olympian/Roman gods reside in Skyland within the Sphere of Gods. Shazam draws from specific gods. Wonder Woman's power partially divine-magical. In *Trials of Shazam!*, Roman gods became "Lords of Magic."
- **Atlantean Sorcery:** Distinct from surface-world magic. Homo Magi built Atlantis, making it one of the oldest traditions.
- **The Green / Parliament of Trees:** Elemental force connecting all plant life. Parliament = former Plant Elementals (like Swamp Thing) who gave up speech/mobility for group mind. Founded by Yggdrasil in Garden of Eden. Part of broader spectrum: The Red (animals), The Clear/Blue (sea life), The Rot (death/decay).
- **Lords of Order and Chaos:** Born from The Source. Originally Good vs. Evil; revised to stagnation vs. anarchy (balance is ideal). **Nabu** (Lord of Order) created Doctor Fate through Helmet of Nabu. Died during *Day of Vengeance*. **Klarion the Witch Boy** — from Witch World; ascended to Lord of Chaos. Created by Jack Kirby in *The Demon* #7 (1973).
- **Blood-cost magic:** Well-established, especially in Constantine titles. Personal cost, blood sacrifice, or entity deals.
- **Rock of Eternity:** Nexus/wellspring of magical power. Shazam's seat of power.

**The World Forge & Barbatos:**
- Perpetua (Multiverse creator) assigned three cosmic beings: Monitor, Anti-Monitor, World Forger (Alpheus).
- Alpheus worked in the Forge of Worlds inside a "sea of possibility." Stable universes -> Orrery of Worlds (positive Multiverse). Unstable ones destroyed.
- **Barbatos:** Dragon created to consume failed universes. Killed the World Forger. Let failed universes fester, corrupting the Forge into the Dark Multiverse. Used Court of Owls as agents. Infected Batman with five Dark Multiverse metals to open a doorway. Defeated and chained in Tenth Metal.

**Batman's Contingency Plans — Full Canon:**
- **Tower of Babel (*JLA* #43-46, 2000):** Created secret files to neutralize every JL member. Ra's al Ghul (via Talia) stole and weaponized them.
  - Martian Manhunter: nanites converting skin to magnesium (catches fire)
  - Aquaman: modified fear toxin inducing hydrophobia
  - Plastic Man: frozen solid, shattered
  - Green Lantern: hypnotic blindness (ring made it real)
  - Wonder Woman: VR nanites trapping her in endless combat
  - Superman: red Kryptonite
- **Aftermath:** JL voted 3-3 on expulsion; Batman left voluntarily.
- **Escalation:** Created Brother Eye and OMACs — even more extreme contingency that went catastrophically wrong.
- **Prime Earth (*Justice League* vol. 2 #20, Geoff Johns):** Showed Superman contingency boxes for each JL member. Also has a plan for himself.
- **Session relevance:** Batman's analytical approach to the System's work — running threat assessments, proposing distributed custody — is entirely consistent with this contingency mindset.
