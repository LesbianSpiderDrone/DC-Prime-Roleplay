# DC Prime Earth Canon Baseline

> **Purpose**: Authoritative reference for the DC Prime Earth setting as it applies to this roleplay. Synthesized from the full canon audit, web research, and compliance checks. Everything here is either verified DC canon or clearly marked as session-specific.

## Key Locations

### The Dark Quarters
- **Canon status**: REAL. Established *JLD* Vol. 2 #1 (July 2018).
- **Location**: Half a mile beneath the Hall of Justice, Washington D.C.
- **Built at**: Wonder Woman's request when she spearheaded the official JLD division.
- **Protected by**: Divine enchantments from Wonder Woman's connections to gods and angelic forces.
- **Contains**: A Morgue (storing bodies under investigation), a Cavern (most dangerous mystical items), a Library ("greatest mystic tomes, teachings, and accessories on the mortal plane" — Doctor Fate).
- **Hall of Justice**: Designed by John Stewart and Wonder Woman, financed by Batman, built by Superman.

### House of Mystery
- Semi-sentient pocket dimension. Reconfigurable rooms, warded.
- **Ownership**: Historically Constantine's (won in poker game from Doctor Occult and Father Time). Zatanna gained it post-Forever Evil when Constantine was ousted as JLD leader. Constantine reclaimed it later. In the Vol. 2 era, team HQ shifted to Dark Quarters; House remains accessible.
- Both Zatanna and Constantine have access in this era.

### Other Canon Locations
- **Gotham City** — Batman's city. Extremely dangerous. Frequent catastrophic events leaving salvage debris.
- **Shadowcrest** — Zatanna's ancestral estate. Magically protected.
- **Belle Reve** — Waller's Task Force X base.
- **STAR Labs** — Premier scientific research institution. Silas Stone works here.

### Critical Interface Problem (Canon-Verified)

The **Apokoliptian-Kryptonian computational interface problem** is a genuine canon challenge, not session-fabricated:
- Apokoliptian tech is powered by Anti-Life Equation energy (via Father Boxes / The Source's dark mirror)
- Kryptonian tech is crystalline-computational (Sunstone paradigm)
- These are built on **opposing cosmic forces** — fundamentally incompatible
- **Cyborg's solution** is unique: Mother Box fragment bonded with human tissue, creating a biological translation layer. Silas Stone built this; no one has replicated it mechanically.
- The session's claim that STAR Labs has worked on this for three years is fabricated *timeline*, but the underlying problem is real.

## Magic Systems (Verified Canon)

### Homo Magi
- Human subspecies evolving parallel to Homo Sapiens (*JLA* #164, 1979)
- Pure Homo Magi don't need spells. Half-bloods (like Zatanna) need incantations to access abilities.
- Decelerated aging (~1,000-year lifespan), superhuman durability
- Taught at the Rock of Eternity. Built Atlantis. Ancestors of modern Atlanteans.
- Many humans carry dormant Homo Magi gene.

### Logomancy (Zatanna's System)
- Backwards-speech spellcasting. Words spoken in reverse manifest as magical effects.
- Non-verbal casting also possible (more difficult, used under duress).
- Length/complexity varies by writer — no formal constraint on spell length.

### Verified Magical Traditions in DC

| Tradition | Institutional Structure | Key Figures |
|-----------|------------------------|-------------|

| **The Green / Parliament of Trees** | Elemental force connecting all plant life. Parliament = former Plant Elementals in group mind. Founded by Yggdrasil. | Swamp Thing |

## Organizations & Institutional Dynamics

### ARGUS (Operational in Setting Window)
- **Full name**: Advanced Research Group Uniting Super-Humans
- Under Department of Homeland Security
- Created after Darkseid's first invasion and JL formation
- **Functions**: JL liaison, Watchtower construction/supply, Red Room and Black Room (supernatural artifact vaults), Belle Reve oversight, parent org for Checkmate and Suicide Squad
- **Leadership history**: Steve Trevor (founding) → Amanda Waller (appointed by President) → Sasha Bordeaux (post-Forever Evil) → Waller again
- **Devastating blow**: Crime Syndicate exposed every ARGUS undercover agent during Forever Evil
- **Destroyed**: Event Leviathan (June 2019) — Mark Shaw teleported ARGUS alongside Checkmate, DEO, Spyral, and Task Force X facilities
- **In our window**: Still operational. Waller is director.

### Government-Metahuman Relations
- DC has no single "registration act" — instead a web of competing agencies (ARGUS, DEO, Checkmate, S.H.A.D.E., later Bureau of Sovereignty)
- **Waller faction**: "metahumans are weapons/threats"
- **Trevor faction**: "work with heroes"
- The Supermen Theory (*Doomsday Clock*): "Why are 97% of metahumans American?" — partially true, DEO did deliberately create some
- Post-Beast World (later era), public opinion turns sharply anti-metahuman

### DEO (Department of Extranormal Operations)
- Tracks metahuman, magical, and alien activity
- Directors: Mister Bones, Cameron Chase
- Learned Batwoman's identity, blackmailed her to find Batman's
- Involved in the "Supermen Theory" — deliberately caused some metahuman origins
- Also destroyed by Leviathan alongside ARGUS

### Court of Owls (Historical, Debris Available)
- Secret society in Gotham using undead Talon assassins (*Batman* Vol. 2 #1-11, 2011-2012)
- Maintained hidden bases throughout Gotham's architecture, underground labyrinth
- Worshipped Barbatos (a.k.a. Tribe of Judas)
- Their exposure of Batman to Electrum was the first step in the "mantling" process culminating in *Dark Nights: Metal*
- Night of the Owls: ALL dormant Talons activated simultaneously, targeting 40+ Gotham figures. Entire Bat-Family fought across city. Massive debris field.

## Batman's Contingency Framework

### Tower of Babel (*JLA* #43-46, 2000)
Secret files to neutralize every JL member, stolen and weaponized by Ra's al Ghul:
- Martian Manhunter: magnesium-converting nanites (catches fire)
- Aquaman: modified fear toxin inducing hydrophobia
- Plastic Man: frozen solid, shattered
- Green Lantern: hypnotic blindness (ring made it real)
- Wonder Woman: VR nanites trapping her in endless combat
- Superman: red Kryptonite

**Aftermath**: JL voted 3-3 on expulsion; Batman left voluntarily. Later escalated to Brother Eye / OMACs (worse).

### Prime Earth Continuation
*Justice League* Vol. 2 #20 (Geoff Johns): Batman showed Superman contingency boxes for each JL member. Also has a plan for himself.

**Session relevance**: Batman's analytical approach to the System's work — threat assessments, distributed custody proposals — is entirely consistent with this contingency mindset.

## Amanda Waller — Threat Profile

### Canon Characterization
- **Backstory**: Son Joe Jr. killed by gang members. Daughter Damita raped and murdered. Husband killed confronting the murderer. Drives her hatred of unchecked power.
- **New 52**: Redesigned younger/thinner. Former Team 7 member. Appointed ARGUS director after Trevor. Founded government-sanctioned JLA (*JLA* Vol. 3 #1, 2013) — each member chosen as counter to a main JL member.
- **Rebirth**: Returned to classic heavyset appearance. Ran Task Force X from Belle Reve. Faked her own death to outmaneuver "the People." Systematically studied Squad members' psychological flaws.
- **Later (post-session window)**: Became Dawn of DC's overarching villain. Used Beast World as political ammunition. Created Bureau of Sovereignty. Part of "Trinity of Evil." In *Absolute Power* (2024), created Task Force VII with Failsafe and Brainiac Queen — six Amazo androids that *permanently steal* metahuman powers. Successfully depowered Superman, Wonder Woman, Flash, GL, Martian Manhunter, Aquaman.

### In Our Setting Window
Waller is ARGUS director with full institutional power. Her interest in acquiring enhanced individuals and exotic capabilities is well-established. The session's 8-month surveillance operation targeting hybrid tech-magic integration is fabricated plot, but entirely consistent with her published character.

## World Forge & Cosmology (Quick Reference)

- **Perpetua** created the Multiverse. Assigned three cosmic beings: Monitor, Anti-Monitor, World Forger (Alpheus).
- **Alpheus** worked in the Forge of Worlds inside a "sea of possibility." Stable universes → Orrery of Worlds. Unstable ones destroyed.
- **Barbatos**: Dragon created to consume failed universes. Killed the World Forger. Let failed universes fester → Dark Multiverse. Used Court of Owls as agents. Infected Batman with five metals to open a doorway. Defeated and chained in Tenth Metal.
- **Element X**: Purest substance in creation. "X" = Roman numeral 10. "Nth" = "Ninth" — making Nth Metal an impure facsimile. Everything in the Multiverse is made from Element X in its least concentrated state.
- **The Source**: Metaphysical force driving the universe. Powers Mother Boxes. Lords of Order and Chaos were born from it.
