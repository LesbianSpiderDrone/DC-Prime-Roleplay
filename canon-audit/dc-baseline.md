# DC Prime Earth Canon Baseline

> **Purpose**: Authoritative reference for the DC Prime Earth setting as it applies to this roleplay. Synthesized from the full canon audit, web research, and compliance checks. Everything here is either verified DC canon or clearly marked as session-specific.

> **Setting Window**: **2018–mid 2019** (post-*Dark Nights: Metal*, during *Justice League Dark* Vol. 2, pre-*Event Leviathan*, pre-Alfred's death). This is the era where all session conditions are simultaneously true.

## Active Roster: Who Is Where

### Justice League Dark (Vol. 2 Roster)
- **Wonder Woman** — Leader. Spearheaded JLD as official JL division. Secured divine enchantments for Dark Quarters.
- **Zatanna Zatara** — Core member. Homo Magi, logomancy. *Not* leader in this era (Wonder Woman is). Key point of contact for magical civilians.
- **John Constantine** — Member. Street magic, occult expertise, connections to magical underworld. Retains connection to House of Mystery.
- **Man-Bat (Kirk Langstrom)** — Member.
- **Detective Chimp** — Member.
- **Swamp Thing** — Associated.

### Bat-Family (Active in Gotham)
- **Batman (Bruce Wayne)** — Active. Contingency planner, intelligence network, analytical methodology. Alfred alive and operational.
- **Oracle (Barbara Gordon)** — Active as Oracle (returned to Oracle identity per *Infinite Frontier* #0, but intelligence role established earlier). Primary information coordinator.
- **Alfred Pennyworth** — Alive. Operational support from Batcave/Wayne Manor.

### Government Apparatus
- **Amanda Waller** — ARGUS director. Acquisition mentality toward metahuman assets. Runs Task Force X from Belle Reve.
- **ARGUS** — Operational. Advanced Research Group Uniting Super-Humans. DHS subdivision. Manages metahuman affairs, JL liaison, supernatural artifact vaults (Black Room, Red Room).
- **Steve Trevor** — Former ARGUS director, removed for WW relationship. Waller replaced him.

### Magical Community
- **Papa Midnite** — Voodoo practitioner, power broker. Transactional relationship with Constantine. Community intermediary.
- **Crazy Jane (Kay Challis)** — Doom Patrol. 64 alters with distinct powers. Relevant as canon precedent for JLD engaging with plurality.

## Key Locations

### The Dark Quarters
- **Canon status**: REAL. Established *JLD* Vol. 2 #1 (July 2018).
- **Location**: Half a mile beneath the Hall of Justice, Washington D.C.
- **Built at**: Wonder Woman's request when she spearheaded the official JLD division.
- **Protected by**: Divine enchantments from Wonder Woman's connections to gods and angelic forces.
- **Contains**: A Morgue (storing bodies under investigation), a Cavern (most dangerous mystical items), a Library ("greatest mystic tomes, teachings, and accessories on the mortal plane" — Doctor Fate).
- **Hall of Justice**: Designed by John Stewart and Wonder Woman, financed by Batman, built by Superman.

### House of Mystery
- Semi-sentient pocket dimension. Reconfigurable rooms, warded.
- **Ownership**: Historically Constantine's (won in poker game from Doctor Occult and Father Time). Zatanna gained it post-Forever Evil when Constantine was ousted as JLD leader. Constantine reclaimed it later. In the Vol. 2 era, team HQ shifted to Dark Quarters; House remains accessible.
- Both Zatanna and Constantine have access in this era.

### Other Canon Locations
- **Gotham City** — Batman's city. Extremely dangerous. Frequent catastrophic events leaving salvage debris.
- **Shadowcrest** — Zatanna's ancestral estate. Magically protected.
- **Belle Reve** — Waller's Task Force X base.
- **STAR Labs** — Premier scientific research institution. Silas Stone works here.

### The Five Divine Metals (All Present in Gotham)

1. **Electrum** — Court of Owls (Year 6). Reanimation metal with trace Dionesium. Cold neutralizes it.
2. **Dionesium** — Endgame (Year 9). Liquid metal, supernatural healing. Found impure in Lazarus Pits.
3. **Promethium** — Batman's resurrection machine.
4. **Nth Metal** — Dark Multiverse viewing machine. Incomplete Element X.
5. **Batmanium** — Court of Owls final trap.

All five exist as trace contaminants in Gotham's soil, water, and infrastructure.
