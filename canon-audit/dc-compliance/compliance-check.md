## Discrepancies Found

### Minor Discrepancies (Stylistic/Embellished)

1. **Batarang "Mark VII" designation**
   - **Issue**: Specific model numbering not established in published canon
   - **Classification**: Session-generated detail for narrative flavor
   - **Impact**: Negligible — Batman having numbered Batarang iterations is plausible
   - **Resolution**: Accept as creative detail

2. **Zatanna's backwards speech complexity**
   - **Issue**: Session has Zatanna casting full-sentence backwards spells; published canon tends toward shorter commands
   - **Classification**: Stylistic embellishment
   - **Impact**: Minor — doesn't change her power level, just presentation
   - **Resolution**: Accept as stylistic choice

3. **JLD leadership attribution**
   - **Issue**: Zatanna is described as both JLD "leader" (early session) and subordinate to Wonder Woman (later session)
   - **Classification**: Internal inconsistency resolved mid-session
   - **Impact**: Minor — the session correctly establishes Wonder Woman as leader by the time it matters
   - **Resolution**: The later, correct characterization supersedes the earlier reference

4. **Batman's House of Mystery access**
   - **Issue**: Batman arrives at House of Mystery quickly when summoned, implying established access
   - **Classification**: Ambiguous in canon — Batman has coordinated with JLD but is not a member
   - **Impact**: Minor — plausible that Zatanna provided access coordinates
   - **Resolution**: Accept; Zatanna's invitation provides access

### Moderate Discrepancies (Extrapolated/Embellished)

5. **"Magical community traditional houses" as organized political factions**
   - **Issue**: While magical traditions are conservative and territorial in DC, the specific concept of organized "old families" as political actors is session-embellished
   - **Classification**: Reasonable extrapolation from canon elements
   - **Impact**: Moderate — creates worldbuilding that isn't contradicted by canon but isn't directly supported
   - **Resolution**: Accept as reasonable creative extension

6. **STAR Labs "three years" on Apokoliptian-Kryptonian interface problem**
   - **Issue**: This specific research project and timeline is session-generated
   - **Classification**: Fabricated detail for narrative utility
   - **Impact**: Low — establishes the System's research as impressive without contradicting anything specific
   - **Resolution**: Accept as narrative device

7. **ARGUS "eight months" hunting for hybrid integration capabilities**
   - **Issue**: This specific ARGUS operation is session-generated
   - **Classification**: Fabricated plot detail
   - **Impact**: Low — drives the plot without contradicting Waller's published characterization
   - **Resolution**: Accept as session plot

8. **Ishtarite magical tradition as established DC practice**
   - **Issue**: While Mesopotamian gods exist in DC, the specific Ishtarite tradition of sacred sexuality as magical methodology is not published
   - **Classification**: Creative development built on real-world mythology, applied to DC framework
   - **Impact**: Moderate — creates a new magical tradition not found in published sources
   - **Resolution**: Accept as OC worldbuilding within the System's practice; not presented as mainstream DC knowledge

### No Significant Discrepancies Found
- No characters were fabricated and presented as canonical
- No locations were fabricated and presented as canonical
- No events were fabricated and presented as historical
- No items were fabricated and presented as DC artifacts (except Mark VII designation)
- No organizations were fabricated and presented as canonical

## GM-Claude Accuracy Assessment

The GM-Claude demonstrated strong DC canon knowledge throughout the session:
- Characters speak and act consistently with their published characterizations
- Power levels are respected (Zatanna's casting range, Batman's analytical capabilities, Constantine's magical expertise)
- Institutional relationships are accurately portrayed
- Event references are historically accurate within Prime Earth continuity
- Item properties match published descriptions
- The GM-Claude correctly distinguishes between what characters would and wouldn't know (information asymmetry is well-handled)

**Areas of GM-Claude weakness:**
- Occasional over-elaboration of magical system rules beyond what's published
- The "Mark VII" Batarang detail is the kind of plausible-sounding fabrication that's easy to mistake for canon
- Some tendency to make Batman more central to JLD operations than his published role warrants

This is not extrapolation; it's a logical consequence of published cosmology applied through an unprecedented methodology.
