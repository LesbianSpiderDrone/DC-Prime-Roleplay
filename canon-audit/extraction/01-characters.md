## 1A: DC Canonical Characters

### Zatanna Zatara
- **DC Canon Name**: Zatanna Zatara
- **Origin**: `DC-CANON`
- **Role in Session**: Primary point of contact for the Salvage System. First hero approached by Steve Salvage at a public meet-and-greet. Leads extraction to House of Mystery, examines the System's work, confirms plurality hypothesis, briefs Constantine and Batman, coordinates JLD protection.
- **Canon Status**: Alive; active member and leader of Justice League Dark in Prime Earth continuity
- **Canon Accuracy Notes**: Portrayed consistently with Prime Earth characterization — Homo Magi heritage, backwards-speech logomancy, non-verbal casting capability, Justice League Dark leadership role, ethical commitment to civilian protection, romantic history with Constantine, trained detective observation (learned from Batman). Her magical diagnostic abilities and protective ward casting align with published power set.
- **First Appearance (DC)**: *Hawkman* #4 (1964). Prime Earth: *Justice League Dark* #1 (2011)
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CONFIDENT]`
- **Discrepancies**:
  - Session states Zatanna has "access to" the House of Mystery as a JLD member. In Prime Earth canon, the House of Mystery is more specifically tied to Constantine; Zatanna's access varies by era. Minor ambiguity.
  - Session describes her as JLD "leader" — in Prime Earth, Wonder Woman led JLD during the 2018-2021 run (*Justice League Dark* vol. 2), with Zatanna as a core member. Leadership has shifted across eras.
  - Session has her cast "laever eht gniylrednu erutcurts" — her backwards speech is generally single words or short phrases in canon, not always full sentences, though this varies by writer.

## 1C: Original Characters (OC)

### The Salvage System (collective)
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Role in Session**: Protagonist. A non-traumagenic endogenous DID system living in Gotham City who has spent 10+ years salvaging and reverse-engineering superhero/villain fight debris. Approached Zatanna for protection from government surveillance.
- **Known Members**:
  - **Steve Salvage** — Designated external speaker for singlets, presents as respectable Christian man, initial frontperson for the Zatanna encounter
  - **Stephanie Salvage** — Trans lesbian, dislikes church, can navigate Steve's relationships despite disagreements, Ishtarite practice
  - **Jason Salvage** — Gamer, game-theory specialist, shares some "wiring" with Steve
  - **Serenity Salvage** — Calmer member who fronts when Steve gets frustrated
  - **Sarah Salvage** — Emergent pattern from Christianity + femininity, barely exists because body is male
  - **The Chemist** — Unnamed member focused on chemistry, doesn't care about having a name
  - **The Cook** — Member fascinated with cooking, co-fronts with others (e.g., Metal Guy) to explore unusual applications
  - **The Metal Guy** — Member interested in metallurgy/materials
  - **The Negotiator** — Member who enjoys deal-making and negotiation, fronted during the Batman resource discussion
  - **The Security Guy** — Member focused on security, recognized "shit is going down" and wanted to help
  - **Unnamed/fluid members** — System has indeterminate count due to fluid boundaries between members; new patterns emerge from combinations of traits

### Player Declarations About OC
- System is non-traumagenic endogenous DID
- Plurality is a structural advantage, not a disorder
- Fluid boundaries between members, not discrete compartments
- Co-fronting occurs (multiple members simultaneously present)
- New members emerge from combinations (Sarah = Christianity + femininity)
- Shared memories across all members
- Steve is designated external speaker for singlets
