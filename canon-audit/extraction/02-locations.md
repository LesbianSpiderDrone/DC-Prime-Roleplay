## 2A: DC Canonical Locations

### House of Mystery
- **DC Canon Name**: House of Mystery
- **Origin**: `DC-CANON`
- **Role in Session**: Primary safe house. Zatanna teleports the System here after extraction. Constantine reinforces its defenses. The System is given workshop space and living quarters.
- **Canon Accuracy Notes**: Described as existing in a pocket dimension, semi-sentient, capable of reconfiguring rooms, unreachable by normal means, protected by ancient wards. These are all canon-accurate properties. The House's "temperamental" personality and ability to grow rooms based on need align with published depictions. Session correctly associates it primarily with Constantine.
- **First Appearance (DC)**: *House of Mystery* #1 (1951). Modern association with Constantine established in various JLD titles.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Discrepancies**:
  - Session describes rooms "marked with a skull" as dangerous — this is a plausible detail but not specifically established in published canon. `[DC-UNCERTAIN]`
  - The House being described as "Constantine's base" is somewhat simplified; the House has its own will and has been associated with multiple characters across DC history.

### Gotham City
- **DC Canon Name**: Gotham City
- **Origin**: `DC-CANON`
- **Role in Session**: The Salvage System's home city. The source of all their salvaged materials from superhero/villain conflicts over 10+ years. The reason they have sophisticated security ("because they live in Gotham").
- **Canon Accuracy Notes**: Gotham's depiction as an extremely dangerous city with frequent supervillain attacks, chemical weapons deployments, and city-wide catastrophes is canon-accurate. The System's characterization of Gotham as a place where security-consciousness is basic survival is well-supported by published canon.
- **First Appearance (DC)**: *Batman* #4 (1940)
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Discrepancies**: None.

## 2C: OC-Associated Locations

### The Salvage System's Apartment / Workshop
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: A Gotham City apartment that serves as the System's living space, workshop, laboratory, art studio, and research facility. Contains multiple workstations, the grimoire, the zettelkasten, preserved specimens, art in dozens of styles, science equipment across multiple disciplines, a dissection/vivisection theatre, the safe with dangerous materials, and extensive research documentation.
- **Session Details**: Visually appears to be used by dozens of people but forensically shows only one person's presence. Contains a magitech security system (hybrid cameras with magical detection), magitech entertainment system, and a motorcycle, van, and dozen supersuits.

### The Safe
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: A heavily secured storage container in the System's apartment with twelve distinct security layers combining various technologies, magics, and hybrid tech-magic systems. Contains the System's collection of materials deemed too dangerous to work with (Joker toxins, Talon compounds, Dionesium, antimatter traces, Apokoliptian fragments, Scarecrow chemicals, biological agents, dark magical artifacts).
