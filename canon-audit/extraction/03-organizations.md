## 3A: DC Canonical Organizations

### Justice League Dark (JLD)
- **Origin**: `DC-CANON`
- **Role in Session**: The primary protective organization. Takes the Salvage System under protection. Zatanna, Constantine, and Batman operate as the JLD response team. Wonder Woman leads and is briefed separately.
- **Canon Roster**: Session implies core membership of Zatanna, Constantine, and Wonder Woman (leader), with Batman as a consultant/liaison. This aligns with the *Justice League Dark* vol. 2 (2018-2021) era.
- **Canon Accuracy Notes**: JLD's mandate as the specialized branch handling magical and supernatural threats, operating independently from the main Justice League, is canon-accurate. The jurisdictional distinction between JLD and main League is well-established in Prime Earth.
- **First Appearance (DC)**: *Justice League Dark* #1 (2011)
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Discrepancies**:
  - Session has Batman deeply involved in JLD operations. In published canon, Batman's JLD involvement varies — he's not a core member but has consulted with them. His level of integration here (immediately responding to Zatanna's call, examining materials, coordinating with JLD) is plausible but may overstate his usual JLD involvement. `[DC-PROBABLE]`
  - The civilian (Steve) is unaware of JLD as a distinct entity, referring only to "the Justice League." Session correctly treats this as a realistic information asymmetry — JLD's existence is not widely known to the general public.

- **Role in Session**: Antagonist organization. Conducting surveillance of the Salvage System under Amanda Waller's direct command. Oracle confirms ARGUS as the surveilling agency with 93% confidence. Has been actively searching for hybrid tech-magic integration capabilities for eight months.
- **Canon Accuracy Notes**: ARGUS as a government intelligence/military agency that monitors and recruits/controls metahumans and enhanced individuals is canon-accurate. Waller's directorship and the agency's interest in acquiring exotic capabilities align with published characterization.
- **First Appearance (DC)**: *Justice League* #7 (2012, Prime Earth)
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CONFIDENT]`
- **Discrepancies**:
  - The specific "eight months of active searching for Element X integration" is session-generated plot detail. `[FABRICATED]`
  - Session references "ARGUS remnants" at one point, suggesting uncertainty about the agency's current operational status, which varies across Prime Earth eras.

### The Salvage System (Internal Community/Governance)
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: The System operates as an internal community with distributed governance. Key organizational features:
  - Designated external speaker (Steve) for singlet interactions
  - Topic-based consultation protocol ("don't ask by name, ask by topic")
  - Shared memory architecture enabling seamless transitions
  - Co-fronting capability for parallel analysis
  - Internal negotiation and consensus processes
  - Fluid membership with emergent patterns based on contextual need
  - Self-described as a "community" rather than a clinical condition
