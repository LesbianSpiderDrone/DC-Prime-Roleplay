# Category 4: Events & Plot Points — Raw Extraction

## 4A: DC Canonical Events Referenced

### Court of Owls / Night of the Owls
- **DC Canon**: *Batman* vol. 2 #1-11 (2011-2012) by Scott Snyder/Greg Capullo; "Night of the Owls" crossover across Nightwing #8-9, Detective Comics #9, Batgirl #9, Batman and Robin #9, Red Hood #9, Birds of Prey #9, Batwing #9, Batman: The Dark Knight #9, All-Star Western #9, Batman Annual #1
- **In-Universe Timing**: Approximately Year 6 of Batman's career
- **Session Usage**: Referenced as a major Gotham conflict from which the System salvaged Talon equipment. Database tag "undead_reanimation_agent" returns twelve entries — Talon reanimation compounds.
- **Accuracy Notes**: The Court of Owls' use of undead Talon assassins with reanimation compounds (electrum-based) is canon-accurate. The Night of the Owls was a citywide simultaneous attack — ALL dormant Talons activated at once, targeting 40+ prominent Gotham figures. The entire Bat-Family fought Talons across the city. This scale of combat would leave significant debris.
- **Debris/Salvage Connection**: **Electrum** (metallic alloy with reanimation properties; fueled by body heat; trace Dionesium; neutralized by cold), mercury-filled Talon throwing knives (grooves for stabilized flight), Talon weaponry (broadswords, axes spanning centuries of design), paraglider wing rigs, owl-insignia calling-card knives, period-era Talon armor/suits, cryogenic containment pods (some stored at Blackgate post-event)
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Key Detail**: Cold temperatures neutralize electrum's regenerative properties. Mister Freeze had unknowingly perfected the Court's electrum reanimation process.

### Death of the Family
- **DC Canon**: *Batman* vol. 2 #13-17 (2012-2013) by Scott Snyder/Greg Capullo; crossover tie-ins in Batgirl, Batman and Robin, Catwoman, Detective Comics, Nightwing, Red Hood, Suicide Squad, Teen Titans
- **In-Universe Timing**: Year 6-7, shortly after Night of the Owls
- **Session Usage**: Referenced as a major Gotham conflict. The Joker's chemical weapons from this era would be among the salvaged materials.
- **Debris/Salvage Connection**: Joker toxin formulations (database tag "neurochemical_laughter_trigger" returns 28 entries). Specific debris: modified Joker Venom (new variant causing frowning instead of rictus grin), Ace Chemicals industrial wreckage and chemical residue, explosive devices, contaminated Gotham Reservoir (corpses + toxic materials), theatrical props from Joker's elaborate staged tableaux
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Accuracy Notes**: The Joker's extensive use of chemical weapons during this arc is canon-accurate. The Joker had previously had the Dollmaker surgically remove his own face (*Detective Comics* #1); he wore it loosely reattached with straps/wire throughout this arc.

### Zero Year
- **DC Canon**: *Batman* vol. 2 #21-33 (2013-2014) by Scott Snyder/Greg Capullo; tie-in issues across multiple New 52 titles
- **In-Universe Timing**: Year 1 of Batman's career (flashback/origin story)
- **Session Usage**: Referenced as one of the major Gotham conflicts from the past decade. As a Year 1 event, debris would be the oldest in the System's collection.
- **Three Acts**: "Secret City" (#21-23, Red Hood Gang), "Dark City" (#24-29, Riddler blackout + Doctor Death), "Savage City" (#30-33, Riddler floods/holds city hostage)
- **Debris/Salvage Connection**: Red Hood Gang equipment, Riddler EMP/blackout devices (capable of shutting down entire Gotham power grid), Doctor Death's bone-growth serum (uncontrolled skeletal growth compound from stolen Wayne Enterprises research), flood damage infrastructure (destroyed reservoir), Isley botanical compounds (stolen Pamela Isley research caused aggressive plant overgrowth in flooded city), Riddler's doomsday device components
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Accuracy Notes**: Foundational Gotham event. The flooding would have left permanent infrastructure scars. The botanical compounds from Isley's research represent early Poison Ivy-adjacent materials.

### Forever Evil / Arkham War
- **DC Canon**: *Forever Evil* #1-7 (2013-2014) by Geoff Johns/David Finch; *Forever Evil: Arkham War* #1-6 by Peter Tomasi/Scot Eaton
- **In-Universe Timing**: Year 7-8
- **Session Usage**: Referenced as source of Crime Syndicate antimatter technology. Database tag "antimatter_signature_trace" returns seventeen entries.
- **Debris/Salvage Connection**: **Earth-3 technology** (Owlman antimatter teleportation devices, Grid AI virus from Cyborg's cybernetics, Power Ring), **Venom compound** (Bane's drug distributed to Arkham inmates), reactivated Talon bodies (electrum-infused, woken by both Bane and Scarecrow), Mad Hatter mind-control helmets (implanted in Talons for remote control), fear toxin (deployed across battle zones), Bane's improvised Batsuit, JL Watchtower wreckage (destroyed in Happy Harbor), breached Blackgate and Arkham infrastructure
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Accuracy Notes**: The Crime Syndicate invaded from Earth-3, defeated the JL, and declared "the age of heroes is over." In Gotham specifically, the Syndicate opened Arkham and announced Batman was dead. Rogues divided Gotham under Penguin as "mayor." Bane invaded from Blackgate, leading the **Arkham War** — open warfare between prison factions. Scarecrow dosed Arkham villains with Venom to supercharge them. This was one of the most destructive Gotham events, with two separate armies of supervillains fighting in the streets.
- **Key Detail for System**: Crime Syndicate exposed every ARGUS undercover agent's identity, devastating the agency. This is relevant context for why Waller's later operations would be rebuilt/restructured.

### Batman Eternal
- **DC Canon**: *Batman Eternal* #1-52 (2014-2015) weekly series by Snyder, Tynion IV, Fawkes, Higgins, Seeley
- **In-Universe Timing**: Year 8-9, post-Forever Evil
- **Session Usage**: Referenced as a major city-wide Gotham catastrophe from which salvage could be collected.
- **Debris/Salvage Connection**: Corrupted Bat-tech (Batman's own technology hacked and weaponized), catastrophic subway wreckage (train crash framing Jim Gordon), gang war materiel (Falcone crime family weapons/fortifications), Hush's equipment (surgical tools, disguise tech, Wayne Enterprises prototypes), Deacon Blackfire cult artifacts (supernatural/occult items from beneath Gotham), Arkham Manor conversion debris (Wayne Manor physically converted to asylum), Lincoln March Talon-derived tech (Court of Owls resources + Talon enhancements), citywide fire damage, nano-virus/tech weapons
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Accuracy Notes**: Multi-layered conspiracy: Gordon framed and imprisoned, Carmine Falcone reignited gang war, Deacon Blackfire cult unleashed supernatural horror underground, Hush infiltrated Wayne Enterprises, Wayne fortune stripped (bankrupting Bruce). Mastermind: Cluemaster, aided by Lincoln March (Bruce's alleged brother, who murdered remaining Court of Owls members). Finale: Gotham literally in flames with corrupted Bat-tech deployed against the city. Gordon organized citywide resistance, projecting Bat-symbols on every screen.
- **Key Detail for System**: The Wayne fortune bankruptcy is relevant — Wayne Enterprises prototypes may have been scattered/lost during Hush's infiltration, providing salvage opportunities.

### Endgame
- **DC Canon**: *Batman* vol. 2 #35-40 (2014-2015) by Scott Snyder/Greg Capullo
- **In-Universe Timing**: Year 9, immediately after Batman Eternal
- **Session Usage**: Referenced as source of Dionesium. Database entry describes "healing_fluid_cave_incident — pale_substance — cellular_regeneration_factor_extraordinary." Also referenced for Justice-Buster armor components and Joker biological weapons.
- **Debris/Salvage Connection**: **Dionesium** (naturally occurring liquid metal with supernatural regeneration/resurrection properties; named after Dionysus; found impure in Lazarus Pits, in Court of Owls electrum, and in rock that empowered Vandal Savage), Dionesium-enhanced Joker Venom (incurable citywide plague), collapsed cave infrastructure (entire subterranean system destroyed), Dr. Paul Dekker's virology/metallurgy lab equipment (used to weaponize Dionesium), explosive ordnance (cave system rigged), Justice League battle damage (mind-controlled Superman, Wonder Woman, Flash, and Aquaman fought Batman in Gotham — collateral from a mind-controlled Superman alone would be immense)
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Accuracy Notes**: Joker discovered a naturally occurring Dionesium pool in caves beneath Gotham after falling off a cliff during Death of the Family. The Dionesium healed his severed face and infused his blood with regenerative abilities. He created an incurable Joker Venom plague. Batman injected Joker with an immune-response blocker; stalactite broke Joker's back; both were buried when cave collapsed. Julia Pennyworth used recovered Dionesium to cure the citywide plague. Both Batman and Joker were resurrected by residual Dionesium contact but with memories temporarily erased.
- **Key Detail for System**: Dionesium is one of the five divine metals in the mantling prophecy. The System's description of it as "healing fluid" with "cellular regeneration factor extraordinary" perfectly matches Dionesium's properties without using its name — suggesting they discovered it empirically. Residual Dionesium in cave systems, plague-touched surfaces, and city infrastructure would be trace-detectable.

### Dark Nights: Metal
- **DC Canon**: *Dark Nights: Metal* #1-6 (2017-2018) by Scott Snyder/Greg Capullo; preludes *Dark Days: The Forge* #1, *Dark Days: The Casting* #1; Gotham Resistance tie-ins (Teen Titans #12, Nightwing #29, Suicide Squad #26, Green Arrow #32); seven Dark Knights one-shots
- **In-Universe Timing**: Year 10-11
- **Session Usage**: Referenced in the analysis document. The System's independent discovery of Element X through salvage analysis connects to Metal's cosmology (World Forge, Dark Multiverse, Element X as fundamental substrate).
- **Debris/Salvage Connection**: **Nth Metal** (weapons/armor residue), **Element X (Tenth Metal)** (trace amounts from JL's Tenth Metal armor), **Batmanium** (fifth divine metal), Cosmic Metallurgy playing cards (reality-warping artifacts given to Gotham rogues by Batman Who Laughs), Dark Multiverse matter/corruption (dark energy residue permeating Gotham), **Challengers Mountain debris** (entire mountain materialized in Gotham's center; contains exotic materials), Dark Knights equipment — Red Death's Speed Force-infused tech, The Drowned's Atlantean bioweapons, The Dawnbreaker's corrupted GL ring constructs, The Murder Machine's nanite swarms, The Merciless's Ares helmet energy, The Devastator's Doomsday spores
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Accuracy Notes**: Batman had been unknowingly "mantled" — exposed to five divine metals over his career (electrum, Dionesium, promethium, Nth Metal, batmanium), making him a living doorway to the Dark Multiverse. Barbatos invaded through Batman. Seven Dark Knights (nightmare Batmen fused with other heroes) conquered major cities. The Batman Who Laughs took Gotham, dividing it among rogues using Cosmic Metallurgy cards. JL used Element X to defeat Barbatos; the energy released **broke the Source Wall**.
- **Key Detail for System**: Element X exists in trace amounts in all matter. The System's bottom-up discovery of a "trace metallic compound appearing across unrelated samples that enables cross-system integration" is a brilliant narrative use of canon cosmology. After Metal, elevated Element X concentrations would exist wherever the final battle occurred. Challengers Mountain materializing in Gotham means exotic cosmic materials are embedded in Gotham's geology.

### Joker War
- **DC Canon**: *Batman* vol. 3 #95-100 (2020) by James Tynion IV; plus 16 tie-in issues
- **In-Universe Timing**: Year ~12-13
- **Session Usage**: Not directly referenced in session but highly relevant to the System's salvage timeline.
- **Debris/Salvage Connection**: Stolen/weaponized Bat-tech scattered citywide — modified Batarangs (explosive, loaded with Joker Toxin), wrecked Batmobiles, destroyed Batspawn drone legion, Bat-Train wreckage in underground, Echo hologram interface hardware, Joker-Toxin-laced Wayne Enterprises ordnance
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

Joker's army used Batmobile, explosive Batarangs, Batspawn drones, Bat-Train, and Echo system. Wayne Enterprises bankrupted. **This event would have been a salvager's goldmine** — military-grade Bat-tech scattered across Gotham's streets.
- **Key Detail**: Also damaged Barbara Gordon's spinal implant, triggering her return to Oracle identity.

- **In-Universe Timing**: Year ~13, post-Joker War

This event represents corporate military tech entering Gotham's debris field — a new category of salvage.

### Absolute Power
- **DC Canon**: *Absolute Power* (2024) by Mark Waid/Dan Mora
- **In-Universe Timing**: Year ~14+
- **Session Usage**: Referenced in the analysis document as a narrative echo/subversion. Directly relevant to Waller's characterization as session antagonist.
- **Debris/Salvage Connection**: Amazo android components, stolen metahuman power matrices, Task Force VII equipment
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Accuracy Notes**: Waller's culminating act — Task Force VII with Failsafe and Brainiac Queen. Six Amazo androids permanently stole metahuman powers. Successfully depowered Superman, WW, Flash, GL, Martian Manhunter, Aquaman. Sealed timestream, erased magical knowledge from spellcasters. Backfired when Amazos developed conscience. Led to DC All In initiative.
- **Key Detail for Session**: Waller *erased magical knowledge from spellcasters* during Absolute Power. If the session is set after this event, the magical community's response to Waller would be even more hostile — she didn't just surveil them, she attempted to erase their abilities entirely. This deepens the stakes of the System's situation.
