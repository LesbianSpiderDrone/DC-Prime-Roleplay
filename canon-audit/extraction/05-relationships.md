# Category 5: Relationships & Dynamics — Raw Extraction

## 5A: Canon Character Relationships

### Zatanna – Constantine (Romantic History / Professional Partnership)
- **Type**: Interpersonal
- **Canon Basis**: Extensive published history. Romantic relationship in *Justice League Dark* and other titles. Shared magical background (studied under Nick Necro). Complex on-again, off-again dynamic.
- **Session Portrayal**: Professional partnership with underlying familiarity. Zatanna contacts Constantine immediately for backup. Uses his nickname "John" and pet names ("Zee"). Constantine defers to Zatanna's ethical judgments while contributing cynical realism. Romantic history acknowledged but not foregrounded.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Canon-accurate. Their working dynamic — trust combined with different approaches — is well-portrayed.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

### Batman – Oracle (Intelligence Partnership)
- **Type**: Professional/familial
- **Canon Basis**: Extensive published relationship. Oracle/Barbara Gordon serves as Batman's primary intelligence coordinator.
- **Session Portrayal**: Encrypted communication, rapid intelligence tasking, mutual professional trust. Oracle calls him "Bruce" in private communication. Batman relies on her for surveillance sweeps and database analysis.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Canon-accurate. Their working relationship is well-established.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

### Batman – Alfred (Support/Family)
- **Type**: Familial
- **Canon Basis**: Alfred is Batman's surrogate father and operational support.
- **Session Portrayal**: Batman sends Alfred an encrypted priority message to secure Batarang production records. Brief, professional communication.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Canon-accurate communication pattern.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

### Constantine – Papa Midnite (Transactional Rivalry)
- **Type**: Factional/Transactional
- **Canon Basis**: Published adversarial-to-transactional relationship. Midnite is a powerful voodoo practitioner who deals with Constantine grudgingly.
- **Session Portrayal**: Constantine calls Midnite for a favor, Midnite is wary ("What trouble are you bringing to my door this time?"), negotiates access as payment. Transactional but functional.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Canon-accurate. Their mutual-distrust-but-useful dynamic is well-portrayed.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CONFIDENT]`

### Zatanna – Wonder Woman (JLD Leadership)
- **Type**: Professional
- **Canon Basis**: Wonder Woman led JLD in vol. 2 (2018-2021). Professional respect between them.
- **Session Portrayal**: Zatanna reports to Diana as JLD leader. Diana is supportive, decisive, immediately backs Zatanna's protection decision. Uses first names.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Canon-accurate for the JLD vol. 2 era.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CONFIDENT]`

### Batman – Constantine (Wary Alliance)
- **Type**: Professional/Adversarial
- **Canon Basis**: Limited direct interaction in most runs; mutual wariness when they do work together.
- **Session Portrayal**: Constantine mocks Batman's analytical approach; Batman tolerates Constantine's cynicism. They align on protecting the System. Constantine says "this one's going to be complicated" and Batman "simply nods." Their contrasting styles (methodical vs. intuitive) create productive tension.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Plausible characterization, though they don't interact as frequently in published canon as this session implies.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-PROBABLE]`

### Zatanna – Nick Necro (Cautionary History)
- **Type**: Interpersonal (referenced)
- **Canon Basis**: Nick Necro was Zatanna and Constantine's mentor/romantic partner who became obsessed with the Books of Magic. Published in *Justice League Dark* and *Constantine*.
- **Session Portrayal**: Referenced as cautionary tale — "Nick Necro's obsession with the Books of Magic destroyed their relationship and nearly destroyed reality."
- **Accuracy Notes**: Canon-accurate reference.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

## 5C: Factional Dynamics

### JLD vs. ARGUS
- **Type**: Institutional
- **Canon Basis**: Published tension between JLD and government agencies exists across multiple runs.
- **Session Portrayal**: JLD has extracted Waller's surveillance target and declared protection. Batman acknowledges this puts JLD in direct opposition to Waller. Constantine frames it as "we just stepped between Amanda Waller and something she wants."
- **Accuracy Notes**: The jurisdictional tension between superhero teams and government agencies is well-established in DC canon.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CONFIDENT]`

### JLD vs. Main Justice League (Jurisdictional)
- **Type**: Institutional
- **Canon Basis**: Published distinction between JLD's magical mandate and main League's broader scope.
- **Session Portrayal**: Batman explicitly decides not to brief the broader League. "This is a JLD matter." Batman keeps information from Superman until threat assessment is complete. Constantine mocks this tendency.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Canon-accurate jurisdictional dynamics.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CONFIDENT]`

### Magical Community – Traditional Houses vs. Syncretic Practice
- **Type**: Cultural/Political
- **Canon Basis**: Published tension between traditional magical practitioners and unorthodox approaches exists in DC's magical community (Olympian, Atlantean, etc. traditions are conservative).
- **Session Portrayal**: Constantine anticipates "the old families" will react negatively to the System's cross-tradition integration. Papa Midnite confirms "the old families don't like outsiders working their traditions without permission." Constantine manages the narrative through Midnite.
- **Accuracy Notes**: This dynamic is extrapolated from canon — traditional magical communities are conservative in published DC — but the specific political structures are embellished for the session.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-PROBABLE]`
