# Category 6: Powers, Abilities & Limitations — Raw Extraction

## 6A: DC Character Powers

### Zatanna Zatara — Logomancy / Backwards Speech Magic
- **Ability**: Casts spells by speaking words backwards. Also capable of non-verbal casting. Homo Magi heritage grants innate magical sensitivity. Can detect magical signatures, scan for contamination/possession, create dimensional pockets, teleport, ward against scrying, and perform diagnostic magic.
- **Canon Basis**: Backwards speech is Zatanna's signature ability, established in *Justice League of America* #51 (1967) and consistently throughout Prime Earth. Homo Magi lineage established in *Justice League Dark* and related titles.
- **Session Portrayal**: Zatanna casts multiple backwards-speech spells throughout the session:
  - "laever ecnallievrus" (reveal surveillance)
  - "laever eht gniylrednu erutcurts" (reveal the underlying structure)
  - "wohs em eht ecruos" (show me the source)
  - "dleihsnus morf gnyrcs" (unshield from scrying)
  - "tropelet su ot [location]" (teleport us to [location])
  - "etaerc a lanoismemid tekcop" (create a dimensional pocket)
  - Multiple ward and shield spells
- **Accuracy Notes**: The range and power of Zatanna's spells is consistent with her published capabilities. She is correctly portrayed as one of the most powerful magic users in the DCU. Non-verbal casting and magical sensitivity are canon-accurate.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`
- **Discrepancies**:
  - Some backwards-speech constructions are grammatically elaborate compared to typical published usage, which tends toward shorter commands. Minor stylistic difference.
  - Session has her cast diagnostic spells like "wohs em sih lacidem etatS" (show me his medical state) — this level of specificity in backwards speech is extrapolated but plausible.

### Batman — Detective/Analytical/Technological
- **Ability**: Peak human physical and mental capabilities. World's greatest detective. Access to advanced technology (Batcomputer, communicator, Batcave resources). Contingency planning. Intelligence network (Oracle, Alfred).
- **Canon Basis**: Core Batman characterization since *Detective Comics* #27. Contingency planning established in *JLA: Tower of Babel* and throughout modern runs.
- **Session Portrayal**: Batman's analytical approach dominates — he examines materials methodically, identifies solved problems, runs threat assessments, contacts Oracle for intelligence, proposes distributed custody. His contingency-minded thinking (Tower of Babel reference) is foregrounded.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Canon-accurate. His level of analytical precision and strategic thinking is well-portrayed.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

### Constantine — Street Magic / Trickery / Occult Knowledge
- **Ability**: Skilled street magician, con artist, and occult expert. Uses manipulation, forced cooperation between magical entities, extensive knowledge of magical traditions. Survival instinct. Connections to occult networks.
- **Canon Basis**: Established in *Hellblazer* and throughout DC titles.
- **Session Portrayal**: Constantine's expertise is demonstrated through his analysis of the grimoire, identification of integration problems he's been working on, and his connections to Papa Midnite and the occult community. His practical, street-smart approach contrasts with Batman's methodical analysis.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Canon-accurate characterization.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

## 6B: OC Powers & Capabilities

### Salvage System — Hybrid Tech-Magic Integration
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: The System's core capability is reverse-engineering and integrating disparate technologies and magical traditions into functional hybrid systems. This is enabled by their plural cognitive architecture — different members bring different analytical frameworks (theological, scientific, artistic, empirical) that allow cross-paradigm problem-solving.
- **Key Capabilities**:
  - Cross-tradition magical integration (multiple pantheons/traditions cooperating rather than subordinated)
  - Alien technology reverse-engineering (Apokoliptian, Kryptonian, Thanagarian, Reach)
  - Syncretic magical framework creation (novel traditions from combined pieces)
  - Property-based analytical methodology (cataloguing by function, not origin)
  - Practical applications: hybrid phones, magitech security cameras, motorcycle, van, dozen supersuits, smart home systems, entertainment systems, grimoire, extensive research documentation
- **Limitations**:
  - Baseline human physical capabilities — sub-street-level combat
  - One body houses entire community — death of body kills everyone
  - No formal magical training
  - No metahuman abilities
  - Knowledge asymmetry: world-destabilizing knowledge but no ability to defend it physically

### Salvage System — Plurality as Structural Advantage
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: The System's DID architecture provides:
  - Parallel processing through co-fronting (multiple members examining same problem simultaneously)
  - Diverse analytical frameworks (theological, scientific, artistic lenses applied to same material)
  - Emergent specialization (new patterns form from combinations of existing traits)
  - Seamless switching (undetectable to trained observers)
  - Shared memory (no information gaps between members)
  - Cognitive diversity without requiring multiple bodies

### Salvage System — Element X Discovery
- **Origin**: `OC` (using canon concept)
- **Description**: Through systematic property-based analysis of salvaged materials, the System independently identified trace amounts of a metallic compound appearing across multiple unrelated samples that enables cross-system integration. They hypothesized this as a "fundamental substrate connecting disparate technological and magical frameworks." This corresponds to Element X from DC canon (*Dark Nights: Metal*).
- **Canon Basis**: Element X is established in *Dark Nights: Metal* as a fundamental creative element from the World Forge.
- **Accuracy Notes**: The System discovering Element X empirically through bottom-up analysis is a novel narrative use of established canon cosmology. The properties attributed to Element X (universal connectivity, cross-system integration) align with its published description.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-PROBABLE]` — Element X's canon properties support this application, but the specific discovery method is session-created.

## 6C: Power Scaling & Interactions

### OC Relative to DC Characters
- **Combat**: Sub-street-level. Cannot fight. One body = community death risk makes any combat unacceptable.
- **Knowledge**: Potentially world-destabilizing. Solved problems STAR Labs and JLD couldn't. Independent Element X discovery. Complete technical analysis of a decade of Gotham threat technologies.
- **Defensive**: Sophisticated magitech security systems, but designed for apartment-level protection, not superhero-tier threats.
- **Strategic**: Exceptional operational discipline. Pre-planned evacuation. Threat assessment capabilities that surprised Batman, Constantine, and Zatanna.
- **Research**: Institutional-grade output from civilian resources. Baseline work with salvage exceeds funded institutional programs. Upper limits with proper resources are unknown.
