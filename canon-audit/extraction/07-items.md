# Category 7: Items, Tech & Artifacts — Raw Extraction

## 7A: DC Canonical Items

### Batarangs (Mark VII)
- **Origin**: `DC-CANON`
- **Canon Description**: Batman's signature throwing weapons made of specialized alloys.
- **Session Description**: Steve found a Batarang at a fight scene, discovered it held an edge extraordinarily well, and began using it as a cutting tool (x-acto knife). This catalyzed the entire salvage operation. Batman identifies it as "Mark VII series, phased out two years ago."
- **Accuracy Notes**: Batarangs are well-established canon items. The specific "Mark VII" designation and alloy upgrade timeline are session-generated details.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CONFIDENT]` (Batarangs = canon; Mark VII = `[FABRICATED]`)

### Mother Box Fragments
- **Origin**: `DC-CANON`
- **Canon Description**: Living computers from New Genesis/Apokolips. Sentient technology that assists its user.
- **Session Description**: Referenced in the System's salvage collection. Analysis document describes Mother Box fragment "choosing cooperation" as a worthiness test. Database contains entries for Apokoliptian technology. Batman mentions Mother Box fragments that won't interface with Earth technology.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Mother Boxes and their sentient, cooperative nature are well-established in *New Gods* and related titles (Jack Kirby's Fourth World). The idea of a Mother Box fragment "choosing" to cooperate is consistent with their published sentient nature.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

### Kryptonian Sunstone
- **Origin**: `DC-CANON`
- **Canon Description**: Kryptonian crystalline technology used for data storage, construction, and computation. Forms the basis of the Fortress of Solitude.
- **Session Description**: Referenced as one of the alien technologies the System has reverse-engineered. Analysis document describes "cross-compiling on alien hardware."
- **Accuracy Notes**: Sunstone technology is well-established in Superman-related titles.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

### Nth Metal
- **Origin**: `DC-CANON`
- **Canon Description**: Thanagarian metal with properties including gravity negation, enhanced strength, and magical conductivity. Connected to Hawkman/Hawkgirl.

- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

### Dionesium
- **Origin**: `DC-CANON`
- **Canon Description**: A chemical compound found in caves beneath Gotham with extraordinary regenerative properties. Kept the Joker alive for potentially centuries. Central to *Batman: Endgame*.
- **Session Description**: Database entry: "healing_fluid_cave_incident — pale_substance — cellular_regeneration_factor_extraordinary." Stored in magically-reinforced cryogenic suspension in the safe.
- **Accuracy Notes**: Dionesium's properties and origin (caves beneath Gotham, connection to the Joker's longevity) are canon-accurate. The System not knowing the official name but accurately describing its properties is consistent with their civilian perspective.
- **Confidence**: `[DC-CANON]`

## 7B: OC-Created Items

### The Hybrid Phone
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: A phone integrating at least four alien technological frameworks with three distinct magical traditions. Contains a custom magic diagram/notes app. Batman identifies seventeen integrated systems (twelve technological, five magical). The first item the System showed Zatanna.

### The Motorcycle
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: A hybrid tech-magic vehicle built from salvaged materials. Referenced in the analysis document as a "discovery vector."

### The Van
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: A vehicle, presumably also incorporating salvaged technology. Listed among the System's assets.

### The Dozen Supersuits
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: Twelve specialized suits, all the same body size, each optimized for different operational profiles (reconnaissance, close quarters combat, research/specimen collection, magical detection/countermeasures, etc.). Built from salvaged materials with hybrid tech-magic integration.

### The Grimoire
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: A magical text containing the System's cross-tradition integration work. Multiple authorial voices, multiple theological frameworks, technical procedures alongside personal theology. Contains solved problems (Olympian-Parliament integration). Described as "novel artifact deriving power from plurality, not unity."

### The Zettelkasten
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: Thousands of index cards with different handwriting, analytical frameworks, and areas of focus. Contains systematic cross-referenced research including the Apokoliptian-Kryptonian interface solution.

### The Magitech Security System (Cameras)
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: Hybrid surveillance cameras with purple-coated lenses and painted symbols that can detect "some types of magic." Standard CCTV hardware modified with twelve technological and five magical integrated systems. Considered "baseline" infrastructure by the System.

### The Magitech Entertainment System
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: Referenced but not detailed. Part of the System's household infrastructure.

### The Magitech Smart Home Assistant
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: Built using "a shard of an alien technology plus a speaker and microphone system." Example Steve gave Zatanna of practical domestic applications.

### The Safe
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: Twelve-layer security system combining various technologies, magics, and hybrid systems. Contains hazardous materials. Only the System can open it.

### Proof-of-Concept Weapons
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: "Magitech guns and knives" that are clearly proof of concept, not made for active use. Demonstrates capability without weaponization intent.

### The USB Database
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: Custom database containing 2,347 catalogued items with 16,209 property correlations. Simple custom-built interface, property-based search, metadata tags. The System's complete research archive organized by functional properties rather than names.

### Research Binders
- **Origin**: `OC`
- **Description**: Boxes of binders, each with custom symbols, documenting individual salvaged materials from dozens of analytical angles. Each binder covers material science, magical conductivity, practical applications, and cross-references to other materials.

No items were presented as canonical DC artifacts that don't exist in published sources. All DC items referenced (Batarangs, Dionesium, Nth Metal, etc.) are genuine DC canon. The "Mark VII" Batarang designation is the only specific detail that appears fabricated.
