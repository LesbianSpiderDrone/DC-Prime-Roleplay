# Category 9: Player Declarations — Raw Extraction

These are statements the player explicitly made about their OC that define the narrative canon of the Salvage System, regardless of what the GM-Claude generated.

---

## Worldbuilding Declarations

### "Non-traumagenic endogenous DID system"
- **Session Location**: Opening premise (line ~9) and throughout
- **Type**: `WORLDBUILDING` / `CHARACTER-TRAIT`
- **Impact**: Establishes the System's plurality as non-pathological — not caused by trauma, emerging naturally from the brain's architecture. This fundamentally shapes how all characters interact with the System and prevents trauma-narrative assumptions.

### "Plurality is a structural advantage, not a disorder"
- **Session Location**: Analysis framework (Section 1, Section 8) and throughout session
- **Type**: `WORLDBUILDING` / `BOUNDARY`
- **Impact**: Prevents any character from treating the DID as something to be "cured." The System's achievements are enabled BY plurality, not despite it. A "cure" would destroy the community and the capabilities.

### "All members front and create their own work"
- **Session Location**: Opening premise
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-TRAIT`
- **Impact**: Establishes that every member is active and productive, explaining the enormous volume and diversity of the workshop's output.

## Character Trait Declarations

### "Steve is the designated external speaker for singlets"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1116, confirmed throughout
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-TRAIT`
- **Impact**: Establishes the communication protocol — Steve handles external interactions with non-plural people. This is why Steve fronts for the Zatanna encounter.

### "Stephanie is trans lesbian, dislikes church"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1219
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-TRAIT`
- **Impact**: Establishes Stephanie's identity and demonstrates the System's internal diversity. Also shows cooperative discipline — Stephanie can navigate Steve's Christian community despite personal disagreement.

### "System members have fluid boundaries (not discrete compartments)"
- **Session Location**: Lines ~1506, ~1532
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-TRAIT`
- **Impact**: Rejects the clinical DID model of discrete alters with firm boundaries. The System is described as overlapping consciousness with areas of specialization — like waves in an ocean, not boxes on a shelf. This is the player's most insistent declaration, correcting all three heroes' assumptions.

### "Co-fronting occurs (multiple members simultaneously present)"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1484
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-TRAIT`
- **Impact**: Explains how the System achieves cross-paradigm integration — different members bring different frameworks simultaneously. The Metal Guy and the Cook fronting together produces "can this armor be used as a cooking surface?" The Christian and the Ishtarite fronting together produces cross-tradition magical integration.

### "New members emerge from combinations"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1532
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-TRAIT`
- **Impact**: Sarah = Christianity + femininity. This establishes that the System is generative — new patterns emerge from existing traits when contextually needed. The System is not static.

### "Some members have no names, just roles"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1484
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-TRAIT`
- **Impact**: The Chemist, The Cook, The Metal Guy, The Negotiator, The Security Guy — these are functional roles, not identities with names. Some don't care about having names.

### "Shared memories across all members"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1116
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-TRAIT`
- **Impact**: Critical for the narrative — no amnesia between switches means no information gaps, seamless continuity, and the ability to function in sustained social situations without confusion.

### "Different people being passed the phone" as switching analogy
- **Session Location**: Line ~1580
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-TRAIT` / `BOUNDARY`
- **Impact**: The System's preferred framework for how outsiders should understand switching. Rapid opinion changes = different people, not instability. The phone metaphor is deliberately chosen as accessible and non-pathologizing.

## Boundary Declarations

### "Do not ask by name — ask by topic/expertise"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1602
- **Type**: `BOUNDARY`
- **Impact**: Establishes the communication protocol JLD should use. Names change, members go dormant, but knowledge persists. Ask "who can help with chemistry?" not "can I speak to The Chemist?"

### "Art/personal cultural work is private, technical research is shareable"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1323
- **Type**: `BOUNDARY`
- **Impact**: Draws a firm line between the System's cultural output (art, poetry, erotic art, theological writings) and their technical research. JLD gets methodologies, integration frameworks, and results. They don't get personal creative work.

### "Salvage was necessity, not preference"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1323
- **Type**: `WORLDBUILDING`
- **Impact**: Reframes the entire narrative — the System didn't choose to work with garbage. They had no safe way to acquire proper materials (contacting dealers = unacceptable risk). Everything they achieved was with the worst possible materials under resource constraint.

### "Not interested in superheroics — can't risk the body"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1219
- **Type**: `BOUNDARY`
- **Impact**: Firm boundary on front-line participation. One body = entire community dies. The risk calculus is fundamentally different from individual heroes. Research contribution only.

### "Willing to destroy dangerous stockpile rather than let Waller have it"
- **Session Location**: Lines ~2583, ~2627
- **Type**: `BOUNDARY` / `PLOT-DIRECTION`
- **Impact**: Establishes the System's priorities — safety over preservation, denial over capitulation. They'd rather destroy the apocalypse box than let it be weaponized.

### "They think about security because they live in Gotham"
- **Session Location**: Line ~2295
- **Type**: `CORRECTION`
- **Impact**: Corrects Constantine's theory that the System had been "hunted before." Their operational discipline isn't from experience with persecution — it's basic Gotham survival. This is simultaneously mundane and deeply telling about life in Gotham.

### "Plan to renegotiate arrangement after immediate crisis"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1602
- **Type**: `BOUNDARY`
- **Impact**: The current arrangement is explicitly temporary. The System intends to renegotiate terms once the Waller threat is addressed. They are not accepting permanent dependency.

## Plot Direction Declarations

### "Research focus, not weaponization"
- **Session Location**: Throughout (lines ~380-463, ~1219, ~1323)
- **Type**: `PLOT-DIRECTION`
- **Impact**: Consistently stated — the System builds research infrastructure, domestic applications, and defensive systems. Not weapons. The proof-of-concept guns/knives are explicitly "not made for active use."

### "Open to research sharing with JLD"
- **Session Location**: Lines ~1219, ~1323
- **Type**: `PLOT-DIRECTION`
- **Impact**: Establishes the System's contribution to the arrangement. They'll share technical methodologies, integration frameworks, and research findings with JLD specifically (not the broader League, not government).

### "Open to some weaponization with strong backer"
- **Session Location**: Line ~1626
- **Type**: `PLOT-DIRECTION`
- **Impact**: Nuanced position — the System isn't categorically anti-weapon, just anti-weaponization-without-protection. With JLD backing, they're willing to explore combat applications of their research.
