# Category 10: Unresolved Threads & Hooks — Raw Extraction

## Plot Hooks

### Waller's Response to JLD Extraction
- **Type**: `PLOT-HOOK`
- **Status**: Open
- **Description**: Amanda Waller has been personally directing ARGUS surveillance of the System for 8+ months. JLD has now extracted her target. Batman predicts she will escalate. All three heroes are preparing for her response.
- **Dependencies**: Oracle's ongoing monitoring of ARGUS communications; Diana's six-hour briefing; JLD defensive preparations
- **Urgency**: High — Waller will realize the extraction within hours.

### Wonder Woman's Six-Hour Briefing
- **Type**: `PLOT-HOOK`
- **Status**: Open (scheduled, not yet occurred)
- **Description**: Diana requested a full briefing within six hours. Zatanna is preparing briefing materials. This briefing will determine JLD's official strategic position and may bring in additional resources.
- **Dependencies**: Zatanna's briefing document preparation; Batman's threat assessment; Constantine's magical community outreach results

### Papa Midnite's Magical Community Management
- **Type**: `PLOT-HOOK`
- **Status**: Open (initiated)
- **Description**: Constantine asked Midnite to spread word through the magical community that the System is under JLD protection. Midnite warned that "the old families" will react negatively to cross-tradition syncretic work.
- **Dependencies**: Midnite's outreach; traditional magical houses' response; Constantine's ability to control the narrative

### Distribution of Dangerous Materials from the Safe
- **Type**: `PLOT-HOOK`
- **Status**: Open (agreed in principle)
- **Description**: Batman proposed distributed custody: Constantine takes magical threats, Zatanna takes hybrids, Batman takes tech/chemical. System agreed and suggested doing it immediately. Specific logistics not yet executed.
- **Dependencies**: Individual containment protocols by each custodian; secure transport methods; ensuring no single location has catastrophic concentration

### Batman's Full Database Analysis
- **Type**: `PLOT-HOOK`
- **Status**: Open (initial examination done)
- **Description**: Batman has copied the database to an encrypted partition. Initial searches revealed 2,347 items with 16,209 correlations, including Element X candidate materials. Full analysis is pending.
- **Dependencies**: Batman's analytical resources; coordination with Oracle; determining what information is actionable vs. archival

## Character Arcs

### The System's Research Capabilities with Proper Materials
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-ARC`
- **Status**: Open (untested)
- **Description**: The System's entire body of work was produced under severe resource constraint. Batman, Constantine, and Zatanna have all recognized that giving them proper materials could produce dramatically superior results. What "advanced" work looks like with proper resources has not been explored.
- **Dependencies**: Material supply chain setup; workshop establishment in House of Mystery; System's research priorities with access to quality materials

### The Salvage System's Actual Upper Limits
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-ARC`
- **Status**: Open
- **Description**: Batman asked "What do you consider your advanced work?" and the System declined to answer ("hold off for a moment"). Their ceiling is unknown. The security cameras were revealed to be "baseline." What constitutes their most sophisticated work remains unrevealed.
- **Dependencies**: System's comfort level with disclosure; practical demonstrations; time and resources

### Long-Term Protection Arrangement Renegotiation
- **Type**: `CHARACTER-ARC`
- **Status**: Open (explicitly deferred)
- **Description**: The System explicitly stated the current arrangement is temporary and they plan to renegotiate after the immediate crisis is resolved. What "better terms" look like is undefined.
- **Dependencies**: Resolution of Waller threat; System's assessment of JLD trustworthiness; practical experience with the arrangement
