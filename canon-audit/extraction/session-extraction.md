The debrief reveals: the System is plural; they've been collecting and systematically cataloguing salvage from a decade of Gotham's catastrophic events; they've independently discovered Element X; they've solved the Apokoliptian-Kryptonian interface problem; and they keep the most dangerous materials in a twelve-layer safe they're willing to destroy rather than cede to Waller.

JLD offers: workshop space in the House of Mystery, a material supply chain (Constantine for magical, Batman for technological), and protection from ARGUS. The System accepts the arrangement provisionally, sharing technical research while keeping personal/cultural work private, with plans to renegotiate once the immediate crisis resolves.

Oracle confirms the surveillance is ARGUS under Waller's direct command — an 8-month targeted operation. JLD has now placed itself between Waller and a specific acquisition target she has been hunting.
