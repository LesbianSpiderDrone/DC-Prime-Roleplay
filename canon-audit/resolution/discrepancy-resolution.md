# Discrepancy Resolution

For each discrepancy identified in the compliance check, this document determines: Is it a GM-Claude error? A deliberate creative choice? An ambiguity that could be resolved either way?

---

## Resolution Key

| Verdict | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| `GM-ERROR` | The GM-Claude stated something factually wrong about DC canon |
| `CREATIVE-CHOICE` | A deliberate departure from or embellishment of canon for narrative purposes |
| `AMBIGUOUS` | Canon doesn't definitively support or contradict the claim |
| `EXTRAPOLATION` | Reasonable logical extension of established canon |
| `OC-WORLDBUILDING` | Part of the player's original creative contribution, not a canon claim |

---

## Discrepancy 1: Batarang "Mark VII" Designation

- **Claim**: Batman identifies the salvaged Batarang as "Mark VII series, phased out two years ago when I upgraded the alloy composition"
- **Canon Status**: No published source establishes numbered Batarang series designations
- **Verdict**: `CREATIVE-CHOICE`
- **Reasoning**: The GM-Claude added this detail to ground the narrative in specificity and establish timeline markers. Batman having iterated Batarang designs is entirely consistent with his character. This is the kind of detail that *should* exist in canon even if it doesn't appear in published sources. It enriches the narrative without contradicting anything.
- **Recommendation**: Retain. Flag as session-specific canon.

## Discrepancy 2: Zatanna's Backwards Speech Complexity

- **Claim**: Zatanna casts multi-word and full-sentence backwards spells (e.g., "dleihS siht nosrep morf lla mrah, gnikcart, dna noisurtni litnu I evomer siht noitatcnoc")
- **Canon Status**: Published backwards speech tends toward shorter constructions, though length varies by writer
- **Verdict**: `AMBIGUOUS`
- **Reasoning**: Zatanna's backwards speech has never been formally constrained to a specific length. Different writers have used different lengths. The session is consistent with the more elaborate end of published usage. This is a stylistic choice, not an error.
- **Recommendation**: Accept. No action needed.

## Discrepancy 3: JLD Leadership Attribution

- **Claim**: Early session references Zatanna as JLD "leader"; later correctly establishes Wonder Woman as leader with Zatanna as core member
- **Canon Status**: Wonder Woman led JLD in vol. 2 (2018-2021); leadership has varied across eras
- **Verdict**: `GM-ERROR` (self-corrected)
- **Reasoning**: The GM-Claude initially misstated Zatanna's role (possibly conflating different eras) but corrected it when the player prompted specific characterization. The correction is the operative characterization.
- **Recommendation**: Use the corrected characterization (Wonder Woman as JLD leader) as session canon.

## Discrepancy 4: Batman's House of Mystery Access

- **Claim**: Batman arrives at House of Mystery when summoned by Zatanna
- **Canon Status**: Batman is not a regular JLD member; his access to the House of Mystery varies
- **Verdict**: `AMBIGUOUS`
- **Reasoning**: Zatanna explicitly summoned him via magical sending. The House of Mystery has admitted non-members before when invited by residents. Batman's access here is contextual (invited for a specific purpose), not implied as standing.
- **Recommendation**: Accept. Zatanna's invitation provides sufficient justification.

## Discrepancy 5: Magical Community "Traditional Houses"

- **Claim**: Organized magical families/houses that react to cross-tradition work as political factions
- **Canon Status**: Magical traditions in DC are conservative and territorial (implied), but organized "old families" as political actors is not well-established
- **Verdict**: `EXTRAPOLATION`
- **Reasoning**: DC's magical community includes territorial practitioners (Atlantean sorcerers, Olympian magic users, Egyptian practitioners) who guard their traditions. The idea that they'd react negatively to an outsider combining traditions without permission is a reasonable inference. The specific political structure is session-created but not contradicted by canon.
- **Recommendation**: Accept as reasonable worldbuilding extension. Note that specific "house" politics are not published canon.

## Discrepancy 6: STAR Labs "Three Years" on Interface Problem

- **Claim**: Silas Stone at STAR Labs has been working on the Apokoliptian-Kryptonian computational interface problem for three years
- **Canon Status**: No published source establishes this specific research project or timeline
- **Verdict**: `CREATIVE-CHOICE`
- **Reasoning**: The GM-Claude created this detail to establish the significance of the System's achievement (solving what institutions couldn't). The underlying problem (Apokoliptian-Kryptonian incompatibility) is reasonable extrapolation from Cyborg's unique biological solution.
- **Recommendation**: Retain as session-specific canon. The underlying incompatibility is a fair inference; the specific timeline is narrative device.

## Discrepancy 7: ARGUS "Eight Months" Hunting Operation

- **Claim**: ARGUS has been actively searching for hybrid tech-magic integration capabilities for eight months under Waller's direct command
- **Canon Status**: No published source establishes this specific operation
- **Verdict**: `CREATIVE-CHOICE`
- **Reasoning**: This is the session's primary plot driver. Waller's interest in acquiring enhanced individuals and exotic capabilities is well-established in canon. The specific operation is session-created to serve the narrative.
- **Recommendation**: Retain as session-specific canon. Consistent with Waller's published character.

## Discrepancy 8: Ishtarite Magical Tradition

- **Claim**: Ishtarite tradition involving sacred sexuality theology as a magical practice exists in DC's magical landscape
- **Canon Status**: Mesopotamian gods exist in DC. Ishtar/Inanna has been referenced. The specific practice described is not established in published sources.
- **Verdict**: `OC-WORLDBUILDING`
- **Reasoning**: This is part of the Salvage System's creative output and identity (Stephanie's practice). It's built on real-world Mesopotamian mythology and applied to the DC framework. It's not presented as something well-known to DC characters (Zatanna doesn't recognize it; it's described as unfamiliar to established practitioners). This makes it OC worldbuilding, not a canon claim.
- **Recommendation**: Classify as OC element. The Mesopotamian pantheon exists in DC canon, but the specific magical tradition is the player's creation.
